What is IoT?

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects – lights, fans, cameras, locks, speakers – that have sensors and software and can connect to the internet to share data and be controlled remotely.

What is a Smart Home?

A smart home uses IoT devices so you can control appliances using a phone app, voice commands, or automatic rules (for example: lights switch on when you enter a room).
